About JC Capital

(01)

A boutique advisory platform built where capital, global brands and visionary development meet.

JC Capital is not a conventional capital-raising firm. We are a strategic business development and advisory platform focused on structuring, accelerating and enabling high-end real estate, hospitality, branded residences and landmark investment projects.

Operating across Brazil, Argentina and international markets, we act as a discreet bridge — aligning developers, investors, funds, global brands and hospitality groups around opportunities of lasting value. Our work is precise, relationship-led and built for projects that aspire to define their category.
